03 — COLOPHON

—

01:00

PLATE

One two-ink riso of a sky, screened at 5.2 px and printed once. It does not move. Its steady-state cost is zero, so it cannot drop a frame on any machine.

MASK

One continuous bone sheet, die-cut. — of it is still paper. Type never sits on the print.

SUBJECT

One live specimen. Procedurally animated, no keyframes anywhere. One load in three is shiny. She is the only thing here that is not printed.

INK

Two inks on bone, covering — of the print, and they are both hers: the sky is the blue she turns when she comes up shiny, and the last warm light is the pink she usually is. The blue is the constant; the second ink is what a project owns.
